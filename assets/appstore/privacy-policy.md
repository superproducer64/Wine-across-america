# Privacy Policy — Pour Across America

**Effective Date:** May 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 1, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Pour Across America ("the App", "we", "our") is a wine intelligence application developed for personal wine journaling, label scanning, and tasting note management. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using Pour Across America, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Display name (optional)
- Password (stored in encrypted form — we never see your plain-text password)

If you sign in with Apple, we receive only the information Apple provides: a unique identifier and, optionally, your name and email address.

### 2.2 Wine Journal Data
When you log a wine entry, we collect the information you provide:
- Wine name, winery, vintage, and region
- Tasting notes (aromas, color, palate, finish, score)
- Grape variety and blend percentages
- Price paid and location where the wine was enjoyed
- Date of tasting
- Label photos you choose to upload

This data is stored in your personal account and is private to you by default.

### 2.3 Label Photos
When you use the label scanner, the photo you take is:
- Sent to OpenAI's API for text extraction (wine name, winery, vintage, and region)
- Optionally saved to your wine entry as a reference photo

Photos are transmitted securely over HTTPS. We do not use your photos to train AI models.

### 2.4 Device Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:
- **Camera** — to scan wine labels
- **Photo Library** — to select existing label photos from your device
- **Location** — to optionally tag where a wine was enjoyed

All permissions are optional. You can decline any permission without losing core journaling functionality. Location data is used only to populate the location field you choose to enter — it is never tracked in the background.

### 2.5 Usage Data
We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking data beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email address | Account authentication and password recovery |
| Display name | Shown within your profile |
| Wine journal entries | Storing and displaying your personal wine history |
| Label photos | Displaying within your wine entries; label recognition via AI |
| Location (if granted) | Populating the location field on your wine entries |

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Supabase
We use Supabase to store your account information and wine journal data. Supabase is a GDPR-compliant cloud database provider. Your data is stored on secure servers and protected by row-level security — meaning only your account can access your entries. Supabase's privacy policy is available at [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

### 4.2 OpenAI
When you scan a wine label, the photo is sent to OpenAI's API solely to extract wine information (name, winery, vintage, region). OpenAI does not use API-submitted data to train its models by default. OpenAI's privacy policy is available at [openai.com/privacy](https://openai.com/privacy).

### 4.3 Apple
If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple processes your authentication. We receive only a user identifier and the information you choose to share. Apple's privacy policy is available at [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:
- **Service providers** listed in Section 4, who process data solely to operate the App
- **Legal requirements**, if we are required to disclose information by law, court order, or government authority
- **Account transfer**, if the App is acquired by another company — you will be notified in advance

The **Share Wine** feature allows you to generate a shareable link for a specific wine entry. Only the wine details you choose to share are included in that link. Sharing is always initiated by you and is never automatic.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Your account data and wine entries are retained for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Upon deletion, all personal data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

Label photos stored in your entries are deleted when you delete the associated wine entry or your account.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Pour Across America is intended for users who are of legal drinking age in their jurisdiction (21 years of age or older in the United States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21. If we become aware that a user under 21 has created an account, we will delete the account and all associated data promptly.

---

## 8. Security

We protect your data using:
- HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our servers
- Encrypted password storage (bcrypt hashing)
- Row-level security on the database — your data is inaccessible to other users
- Secure, private cloud storage for uploaded photos

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.
