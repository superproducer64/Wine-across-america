# How To Get Build Reports — Quick Reference Guide

Use these prompts at any point during or after a session to capture progress and generate reports.

---

## At the START of a Session

Say this to label your work clearly from the beginning:

> "Today we are working on [feature name or goal]. Please track what we accomplish."

This ensures the session has a clear purpose that shows up in the history.

---

## During a Session

To check in on what's been done so far:

> "What have we built so far today?"

> "Give me a quick summary of what's been completed this session."

---

## At the END of a Session

This is the most important habit. Always close out a session with one of these:

> "Wrap up today's session with a detailed summary of everything we accomplished."

> "Save a progress report for today — what was built, what changed, and what's next."

> "Write a session report covering what was done today and any open items."

This creates a rich checkpoint in the build history that can be referenced in future reports.

---

## To Generate a Report at Any Time

### Full project history (like the one above):
> "Generate a full development report from the beginning of the project to today."

### A specific date range:
> "Give me a report of everything built between [date] and [date]."

### A single session or day:
> "What was accomplished on April 15th?"

### A downloadable version:
> "Create a downloadable report of everything we've built so far."

### A feature-specific summary:
> "Give me a summary of how the label scanning feature was built."

---

## To Track Time More Accurately Going Forward

The build history records *when* saves happen, but not active working hours.
To get more precise time tracking, say at the start of each session:

> "Note that we are starting work now at [time]. Track this session."

And at the end:

> "Note that we are ending this session at [time]. Save a summary."

This creates a timestamped log you can reference for billing or reporting purposes.

---

## Milestone Markers

When a major feature or phase is complete, say:

> "Mark this as a milestone — [name it, e.g. 'MVP Complete' or 'Client Demo Ready']."

This tags the moment clearly in the project history.

---

## Example Full End-of-Day Prompt

> "We're done for today. Please wrap up with a session report covering what was built,
> any issues resolved, what's pending, and save a downloadable version."

---

*Keep this guide handy at the start and end of every build session for the cleanest reporting record.*
